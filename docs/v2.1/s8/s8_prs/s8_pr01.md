# PR-01: Invocation-Owned Raw Capture and Replay Baseline

## Goal

Make Claude, Codex, and Cursor raw stdout and stderr durable under invocation ownership and establish the replay baseline for later converter work.

## Builds On

Slice S7 merged baseline and current daemon-owned invocation lifecycle flows.

Companion fixture protocol: `docs/v2.1/s8/s8_fixture_matrix.md`
Captured direct-runner corpus: `docs/v2.1/s8/s8_fixture_capture_20260312.md`

## Acceptance

- Landed and discarded supported invocations retain their original raw stdout and stderr without depending on a surviving sandbox directory.
- Raw log read behavior continues to work after lifecycle cleanup for supported invocations.
- Preserved raw output remains suitable as replay input for future converter changes rather than being coupled to the current parser shape.
- The capture model preserves enough fidelity that future parser changes do not require reproducing old invocations to recover missing information.
- A documented fixture-capture protocol exists for collecting current real Claude, Codex, and Cursor outputs for replay and regression use.

## Non-Goals

- No cross-surface turn or activity convergence in this PR.
- No major converter behavior changes beyond compatibility needed to keep raw capture and read paths coherent.
- No visual redesign of history, checkpoint, or watch surfaces.

## Key Decisions (if any beyond L2)

**Raw capture is authoritative**: this PR treats preserved runner stdout and stderr as the durable authority path that later normalized and projected views can replay from.

**Replayability outranks current parser convenience**: the stored capture must remain useful even if runner output changes shape later.
