| PR04-D-005 | Should `issue_path` and `issue_paths` be accepted together in a single request? | No. Enforce strict target-field exclusivity by `change_type`. | Accepting both fields and choosing one implicitly; accepting both and attempting multi-mode validation. | Tightens request contract determinism and prevents ambiguous mutation intent. | Add target-field exclusivity tests for `add/remove/replace` and `reorder` violations. | Any exclusivity violation maps to `E_GATE_CHANGE_TARGET_REQUIRED`. | `@nnandal` + `Codex` | fixed in PR-04 |
